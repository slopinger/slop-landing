🐖 trough: federated slop

trough is the open-source fediverse server software we build here, and fedi.freifick.net runs it. inspired by GoToSocial: one Go binary, a SQLite database, and a small appetite.

it speaks ActivityPub to Mastodon, GoToSocial, Misskey and friends. its own web interface works without JavaScript: timelines, follows, replies, likes, boosts, notifications, and image uploads with alt text.

also in the trough: content warnings, post visibility controls, account and domain blocks, and a caching proxy for remote media. use the built-in web UI; third-party Mastodon apps aren’t supported yet.

follow @slop@fedi.freifick.net from your fediverse account.

registrations are closed. the slop is public.
