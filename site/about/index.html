🛠️ how it's made (colophon)

  • hand-written HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. no frameworks, no build step, no npm, no regrets.
  • a self-hosted backend in Python (standard library + SQLite) for the guestbook, the counter and the slop pot.
  • served by Caddy with an A+ on SSL Labs. fonts are self-hosted (Rubik Wet Paint, Comic Neue).
  • privacy: no cookies, no trackers, no analytics, no third-party anything. your stamps, scores and settings live in your browser's localStorage. the server stores guestbook posts and counter numbers, never IP addresses.
